# Pactics Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Pactics** (the "Game") collects, uses and shares information when you play it on iOS or Android.

## 1. Who we are

Pactics is developed and published by **Yufen Quan**, an individual developer, who also publishes on Google Play under the name **WheatGames Studio** ("we", "us", "our"). We are responsible for the personal information processed through the Game.

Contact: **wheatgames123@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

Creating an account and playing the Game does not require your real name, email address or phone number, and the Game does not access your contacts, photos or precise location. Your account is identified by a Player ID. If you contact us, we also process the information you choose to send us (see d) below). The Game collects the following information:

**a) Account and game data**

- Player identifiers created when you first open the Game (a PlayFab player ID, which is your Player ID, and a Firebase user ID).
- If you choose to sign in with **Game Center** (iOS) or **Google Play Games** (Android): your platform player ID and display name, used to link and restore your account.
- A device identifier used to sign you in as a guest (on iOS this is the identifier for vendor, IDFV; on Android an identifier derived from the Android ID).
- The nickname you choose in the Game. **Your nickname is visible to other players**, for example when your team appears as an opponent or on leaderboards, together with your ranking or score, so please do not use your real name or other personal information as your nickname.
- Your game progress (cloud save), such as levels, items and currency balances.

**b) Purchase data**

- If you make an in-app purchase: the products you bought, purchase dates and transaction receipts. Payments are processed by Apple or Google. **We never receive your payment card details.**

**c) Device and usage data (collected automatically by the services listed in Section 5)**

- Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Google Advertising ID on Android).
- IP address and approximate location derived from it (country/region level).
- Device model, operating system version, language, network type and similar technical information.
- How you use the Game, such as sessions and in-game events, and how you interact with ads.
- Crash reports and diagnostic data.

**d) Information you send us**

- If you contact us by email (for example, for support or to delete your account): your email address, the content of your message and anything you choose to attach, such as your Player ID, purchase order numbers, device model, screenshots or screen recordings. Please do not send us payment card details or government ID documents; we never need them.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide the Game, keep your progress saved and let you restore it on a new device.
- To show your nickname to other players in the Game, such as in matches and on leaderboards.
- To process and restore in-app purchases.
- To show rewarded video ads, including personalized ads where you have allowed it.
- To understand how the Game is played and improve it (analytics).
- To find and fix crashes and bugs.
- To prevent fraud and cheating and keep the Game secure.
- To answer your support requests, verify that an account belongs to you, and handle your privacy and account deletion requests. We use the information you send us only for these purposes.

## 4. Advertising, tracking and your choices

The Game shows ads through **Appodeal** and its advertising partners. These partners may use your advertising identifier and device information to show personalized ads and measure ad performance.

- **iOS:** We ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your IDFA can be used for tracking. You can change this at any time in *Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking*.
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** We ask for your consent through a consent form before your device identifiers are used for advertising. Depending on the choices you make, you may see personalized ads, non-personalized ads or limited ads, and in some cases rewarded ads may not be available. Non-personalized ads are not based on your past behavior, but may still use device identifiers for purposes such as limiting how often you see an ad, fraud prevention and aggregated ad reporting, where permitted by your choices and applicable law.
- **United States:** You can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising through **Settings > Privacy Settings** in the Game, where this option is available in your region.
- **Android:** You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time from **Settings > Privacy Settings** in the Game.

## 6. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

- **Performance of a contract:** to provide the Game, your account, cloud save and purchases.
- **Consent:** for personalized advertising and tracking, and for analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). Analytics stays off until you have made your choice in the consent form, and is only turned on if you allow information to be stored on and accessed from your device. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy Settings.
- **Legitimate interests:** for crash reporting, fraud prevention and security, and to answer your support requests.
- **Legal obligation:** to handle your privacy and account deletion requests, and to keep purchase records where the law requires it.

## 7. Data retention

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize the data linked to your account within 30 days, including the data that our service providers store for us (PlayFab, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, and RevenueCat), and we ask those providers to delete it as well. We may keep certain records (for example, purchase records) where the law requires it.

Analytics, crash reporting and advertising data is not linked to your Player ID or account. It is tied to app-instance or device identifiers. Its retention and deletion are governed by the privacy policies of Google Firebase, Appodeal and its advertising partners, and by applicable law.

Emails and attachments you send us are kept while we handle your request and for up to 24 months after it is resolved, so that we can follow up on related questions, and are then deleted. Records showing that we completed a privacy or deletion request may be kept longer where needed to prove that we handled it.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California and other U.S. state residents may also have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and will not be discriminated against for exercising their rights.

To exercise these rights, email **wheatgames123@gmail.com** and include your **Player ID** (shown on your Player Profile screen in the Game) or the order number of an in-app purchase, so we can find your account. If you have neither, contact us anyway and we will try to identify your account with the information you provide. To delete your account and data, see [Delete your account]({{ '/delete-account.html' | relative_url }}).

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 9. International transfers

Our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 11. Children
