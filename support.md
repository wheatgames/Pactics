# Pactics Support

**Pactics** is developed by **Yufen Quan** (WheatGames Studio on Google Play). If you need help with the Game, we are happy to help.

## Contact us

Email: **wheatgames123@gmail.com**

To help us find your account quickly, please include:

- Your **Player ID**, which is shown on your **Player Profile** screen and in **Settings > Manage Online Account** in the Game. If you cannot open the Game, include the order number of an in-app purchase instead, if you have one.
- Your device model and operating system version (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 18).
- A short description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot.

We usually reply within a few business days.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I keep my progress or move it to a new device?

Sign in with **Game Center** (iOS) or **Google Play Games** (Android) in the Game. Your progress is saved to the cloud and linked to that account. On your new device, install the Game and sign in with the same account to restore your progress.

If you only play as a guest without signing in, your progress may be lost if you uninstall the Game or change devices.

### I paid for an item but did not receive it.

First, make sure you have a stable internet connection and restart the Game. If the item still does not appear, email us with your Player ID, the item you bought and the purchase receipt or order number from the App Store or Google Play.

### How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds must be requested from the store where you made the purchase:

- **App Store:** [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/)
- **Google Play:** [Google Play refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)

### How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open **Settings > Privacy Settings** in the Game. On iOS, you can also change tracking permission in *Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking* on your device.

### How do I reset my progress?

Open **Settings > Manage Online Account > Reset Progress** in the Game and type RESET to confirm. Your progress on your device and in the cloud will be erased; your Player ID, nickname and settings are kept. Purchased gems are not restored after a reset. Resetting does not delete your account.

### How do I delete my account?

See [Delete your account]({{ '/delete-account.html' | relative_url }}).

### I found a bug.

Please email us with your Player ID, your device model and a description of what happened. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.
